# Compare Internet Plans - Next.js Landing Site

A transparency-first internet-plan comparison website designed for paid-search landing pages.

## Setup

1. Copy `.env.example` to `.env.local`.
2. Replace **every placeholder** with the exact business information you use for Google Ads advertiser verification.
3. Install dependencies: `npm install`
4. Run locally: `npm run dev`
5. Before launch, verify every navigation link, phone number, email address, legal page, and mobile layout.

## Critical production checklist

- Use your exact legal business name or a properly verified brand/domain name.
- Put the same business identity prominently on the landing page and in Google Ads.
- Use a phone number that belongs to, or is properly associated with, the advertised business.
- Do not claim to be an ISP's "official support", "customer service", or authorized seller unless you can document that relationship.
- Do not use provider logos without permission. This template intentionally uses text names only.
- Keep the landing page functional, crawlable, mobile-friendly, and free of forced popups or deceptive redirects.
- If your business model changes from independent comparison to authorized reseller, update the disclosure to exactly match the documented relationship.
- Have a qualified attorney review the privacy policy and terms for your actual data practices and jurisdictions.
